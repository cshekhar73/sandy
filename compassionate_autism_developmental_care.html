Our Clinical Specializations

Holistic Services Built Around Every Child's Unique Strengths

Inspired by evidence-based care models, we treat every individual as a whole person, supporting communicative autonomy, emotional regulation, and joyful community participation.

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

Positive reinforcement and neuro-affirming behavior plans guided directly by Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) and skilled Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs).

  • Early intervention (Ages 18mo - 6yrs)
  • Emotional regulation & coping tools
  • Reduction of high-intensity behaviors

In-Home & Community Living

Real-world learning where life happens. We help youth and young adults develop self-help routines, mealtime tolerance, hygiene habits, and grocery outing skills.

  • Morning & bedtime transitions
  • Independence in public spaces
  • Community volunteerism & integration

Speech & AAC Communication

Giving every individual their authentic voice through expressive speech practice, non-verbal gestures, and Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC) devices.

  • High-tech AAC device setup (Proloquo, TouchChat)
  • Functional request training
  • Feeding & oral sensory integration

Social Groups & Peer Play

Nurturing genuine friendships in small, neurodiversity-affirming peer pods. Structured activities include cooperative gaming, sports, board games, and conversational turns.

  • Matched by age and mutual interests
  • Perspective taking & teamwork
  • Weekend sensory-friendly gatherings

Caregiver Coaching & Respite

Parents cannot pour from an empty cup. We offer individualized parent training, sibling empathy workshops, and respite coordination to restore family peace.

  • De-escalation & co-regulation strategies
  • IEP meeting advocacy guidance
  • Medicaid Waiver (DD Waiver) navigation

Diagnostic & Clinical Consults

Assisting families with screening evaluations, ADOS-2 behavioral observations, and functional behavior assessments (FBA) to unlock state and medical benefits.

  • Clear diagnostic pathway assistance
  • Comprehensive functional assessments
  • Fast-track pediatric referrals

Our Care Philosophy

We Believe In Meeting Children Where They Are, Not Forcing Conformity.

Every child’s brain is wired with extraordinary value. At BeaconCare, we reject rigid, compliance-only approaches. Instead, our behavioral health specialists incorporate trauma-informed, playful, and autonomy-first strategies that preserve each individual’s dignity.

Child-Led Play & Assent

Therapy only moves forward when the child feels safe, heard, and voluntary in the activity.

Family-Centered Collaboration

Parents aren't observers—they are vital co-leaders in setting clinical goals and tracking wins.

Advocacy with Schools & Doctors

We attend IEP meetings and collaborate directly with pediatricians for synchronized developmental growth.
